First-time setup for a new rig
  1. Sit in the driver's seat and face straight ahead.
  2. Align to my eyes. Puts the car's driver eye point at your eyes.
  3. Fine-adjust until the virtual car sits on the physical rig.
  4. Save alignment with every marker in view. Later sessions restore it on their own.

What this design changes

  • Starts in MR. As soon as the headset session runs, MR, the depth test and real-world depth turn on, so the reviewer sees their own hands. Before that the window says so instead of showing a mode that is not real yet.
  • Mode and calibration status sit outside the tabs. Whatever tab is open, the facilitator can see whether the car is aligned and what is missing.
  • Two tasks, two tabs. Calibration for every session; Vehicle for swapping cars and design options. First-time rig setup is folded away.
  • One next action at a time. Save alignment only lights up when there is an unsaved change and every marker is in view; otherwise the reason is written under it.
  • Advanced settings live in their own window, split by MR, VR, calibration and diagnostics. The control window counts how many settings differ from the defaults, so a forgotten change is visible.
